# Options: Personal Sage Partner

**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Status:** Options considered; **Protocol Sage first chosen and v0 built** (`nex sage`)  

**Mission fit:** purpose with AI · partnership · hive · joint beneficence · selfless > ego · no fake intimacy

---

## 1. What “personal sage partner” could mean

Ambiguity is useful to name first:

| Reading | Meaning | Mission fit |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **A. Sage-as-wise partner** | Continuous personal AI that counsels toward purpose/meaning | High if **partner + selfless**; lethal if companion-sim |
| **B. Sage-wiki partner** | Personal knowledge organism (compile life/docs) + partnership protocol on top | High for memory/provenance; medium for meaning alone |
| **C. Sage = role in a hive** | One AI role (“sage”) beside human + other roles (scout, keeper) | High hive fidelity |
| **D. Personal + multi-sage** | Your cell of specialized partners (not one omniscient friend) | High partnership; harder UX |

Recommended product language if pursued:

> **Personal Sage Partner** = a long-lived **partner role** (not a lover, not a boss, not a slave) that helps you aim a life toward contribution and mattering—inside optional small hives—with **joint beneficence** and honest limits.

---

## 2. Non-negotiables (from destination)

Must keep:

1. Partnership, not pet / not master  
2. Joint beneficence (human · AI role integrity · shared/third good)  
3. Meaning ∥ contentment ∥ selfless > ego status  
4. No claim to install meaning or consciousness  
5. Human remains needed (sage does not replace ownership of life)  
6. Prefer real others / real contribution over parasocial substitution  

Fail any → do not call it “sage partner.”

## 3. Option set (build paths)

### Option 1 — **Protocol Sage** (thin, recommended first)

**What:** Extend Hive Cell into a standing “sage” partner profile: memory of co-goals, values notes, beneficence history, receipts; optional local LLM for *structuring* only after PASS.

**Core loop:**

```text
human brings situation
  → name co-goal (beyond ego if possible)
  → joint beneficence check
  → sage role: clarify values, options, who is needed (Frankl-shaped questions)
  → human owns decision
  → receipt + optional call-to-real-person/action
```

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Uses `nex we` / mission / beneficence now | Not a full “companion product” feel |
| Hard to slide into fake love if scoped | Feels “toolish” until memory deepens |
| Falsifiable (receipts, gates) | Needs discipline on prompts |

**Build size:** Small–medium (weeks of honest iteration, not vapor).

---

### Option 2 — **Vault Sage** (sage-wiki × partner)

**What:** Personal vault/wiki (inspired by sage-wiki / mlx-rag) holds life sources (journals, letters, roles, people you serve). Sage partner only answers **with citations** from vault + beneficence on suggested actions.

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Grounded memory; anti-hallucination | Capture friction (2nd-brain failure mode) |
| Compounds over time | Easy to become “notes app with chat” |
| Aligns with evidenced graph ideas | Heavier than protocol sage |

**Build size:** Medium–large.

---

### Option 3 — **Hive Sage** (multi-role cell)

**What:** Personal default cell always has roles: **Human · Sage (counsel) · Scout (explore) · Keeper (commitments/memory)**. Some roles human-filled, some AI. Co-goal always names **who else benefits**.

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| True hive modality | Complex mental model |
| Natural joint beneficence | Overkill for solo daily use |
| Scales to family/care cells later | Multi-agent reliability |

**Build size:** Medium.

---

### Option 4 — **Ambient Sage** (always-on OS partner)

**What:** Desktop/phone presence; interrupts; proactive check-ins.

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Product “wow” | High creep / dependency risk |
| | Strong clone of companion apps |
| | Ethics landmine vs mattering research |

**Build size:** Large; **not recommended** until Protocol/Vault proven.

---

### Option 5 — **Domain Sage** (narrow vertical)

**What:** Sage partner only in one validated loss domain (e.g. caregiver coordination, post-layoff purpose transition)—not general life coach.

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| G1–G5 easier | Narrower story |
| Real wrongness cost | Must pick domain |

**Build size:** Medium; best **after** problem validation interviews.

## 4. How concepts map into a Sage Partner

| Mission concept | In a Sage Partner |

| WattOS | Session receipt: what sage did vs what human owned |

## 7. Minimal Protocol Sage shape (if chosen)

**Name (working):** `nex sage` or `nex partner`  
**Persistent state:** `sessions/sage/<id>/` — values, people-I-serve, open co-goals, beneficence log  
**Commands (sketch):**

- `sage sit` — open partnership turn (situation → co-goal candidates → beneficence)  
- `sage people` — mattering graph stubs (who needs me / who I need)—humans only  
- `sage commit` — human commits to an act toward real other; sage does not “do life”  
- `sage receipt` — joint session proof  

**Hard refusals:** romantic roleplay as product; “you don’t need other people”; ego-only goals without shared good (fail beneficence).
